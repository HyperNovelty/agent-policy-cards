# Code of Conduct

This project is a small public workspace for static policy-card examples,
validators, and review-only governance artifacts. Participation should help keep
the work clear, bounded, and safe to inspect.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific in issues, reviews, and pull requests.
- Prefer evidence, narrow claims, and reproducible examples.
- Keep examples public-safe and synthetic unless a source is clearly public.
- Respect the project's safety boundary: no secrets, no credential requests, no
  account actions, and no attempts to turn static cards into runtime authority
  without separate review.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety wording, and repository hygiene.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Posting secrets, credentials, private data, or account material.
- Pressuring contributors to publish, deploy, spend money, contact third parties,
  or take external account actions.
- Misrepresenting these cards as production enforcement, certification, legal
  advice, or proof of adoption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions that violate this
conduct note or the repository safety policy. Repeated or serious violations may
lead to blocked participation.

Report conduct concerns through the repository owner's normal maintainer contact
or moderation process. Do not include private sensitive material in public issues.
