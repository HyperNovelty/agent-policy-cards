# Verifier Checklist

Packet ID: `[packet_id]`  
Reviewer: `[name]`  
Date: `[YYYY-MM-DD]`

## Source ledger

- [ ] Every source has URL or explicit non-URL origin.
- [ ] Every source has a source-state label.
- [ ] Local evidence paths exist or limitation is stated.
- [ ] Private/internal sources are not marked public-ready.

## Claim ledger

- [ ] Every non-trivial factual claim has at least one source ID.
- [ ] Interpretation is separated from source claim.
- [ ] Verification status is explicit.
- [ ] Public-use status is explicit.
- [ ] Broad claims have caveats or corroboration needs.

## Visual evidence

- [ ] Screenshots/renders/charts are tied to source IDs and claim IDs.
- [ ] Visual limitations are stated.

## Public-risk crossing

- [ ] No unsupported legal, financial, health, safety, income, trading, or professional claim.
- [ ] No credential/account/private-client material copied into packet.
- [ ] Publishing/uploading/sending/scheduling remains blocked until explicit approval.

- [ ] `approved_for_draft_use`
- [ ] `approved_for_public_use`
- [ ] `needs_more_sources`
- [ ] `blocked`
- [ ] `archived`
